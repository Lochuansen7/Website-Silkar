About Us

PT Silkar National is a trusted Indonesian construction and engineering company with a strong track record in delivering infrastructure, mechanical, electrical, and telecommunication projects. Since our establishment, we have grown from small-scale electrical installations into a multi-disciplinary contractor capable of handling large and complex developments.

Our core business includes power generation and distribution systems, civil construction, mechanical installations, and network infrastructure. We have successfully completed projects ranging from power stations and transmission networks to bridges, roads, irrigation systems, and telecommunication installations, including fiber optic and satellite-based technologies.

Decades of experience have shaped us into a reliable partner for both public and private sectors. Our team consists of skilled professionals who are committed to quality, precision, and customer satisfaction. With a strong foundation in engineering, we bring technical expertise and practical solutions to every project.

At Silkar National, we believe people are our greatest asset. Continuous development and a culture of innovation drive our ability to adapt and deliver excellence in a constantly evolving industry.
