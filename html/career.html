Open Position :
Financial Reporting Officer

Kualifikasi:

  • Usia : Below 30 years old
  • Jenis Kelamin : Male or Female
  • Pendidikan : Bachelor’s degree in Accounting
  • Pengalaman : Minimum 3 years experience in financial reporting

Skill

  • Strong knowledge of financial regulations (IFRS/PSAK)
  • Strong analytical, critical thinking, and communication skills
  • Advanced Excel and data analysis skills
  • Proficiency with Accounting Software

Job Desk:

  • Prepare and review monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements in compliance with IFRS/PSAK.
  • Analyze financial performance against budget/forecast and historical trends.
  • Provide insights and commentary on significant fluctuations and key metrics.
  • Liaise with other departments (e.g., tax, procurement, project) for data gathering and reporting alignment.
